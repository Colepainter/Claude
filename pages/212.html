Designed for shared rituals and intentional gatherings, the 212° offers the full spectrum of traditional dry sauna benefits in a sophisticated open layout. Comfortably seating 6–8, it delivers the perfect blend of luxury and value — a spacious, high-quality sauna offering a rejuvenating escape.

Stretch Out.

Multi-level floating benches give you the freedom to personalize your experience — find your ideal heat level, practice stretching, or simply lie back and surrender to the warmth. Extra bench width makes yoga and full-body stretching a natural part of your ritual.

Open up the space and bring nature into every session. Panoramic glass frames your surroundings — whether it's a mountain ridgeline, a backyard garden, or an open sky — transforming each session into an immersive experience.
