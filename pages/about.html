Our Philosophy

Through Heat and Cold,
We Find Peace in the Discomfort

At New Primitive, we design modular wellness environments built around the primal experience of heat, cold, and recovery. These spaces invite regulation, presence, and return — not as protocol, but as lived experience. A ritual to come back to, again and again.

We build sauna and cold plunge spaces that mirror a transformative process. The heat opens you. The cold brings you back. The stillness in between is where the shift happens.

Our planetary pledge: we use only natural, biodegradable, decarbonizing materials and minimal-impact foundations. We revere nature — and our spaces reflect that reverence in every detail.

The Build

80,000 sq ft. One Focus. Thermal Spaces.

We operate out of an 80,000 sq ft facility in Utah, specializing in modular architecture. Our in-house team focuses exclusively on thermal spaces and cold plunge systems — it's all we do, and we do it at a high level.

Working alongside our trusted partner Polaris Modular, we create wellness environments that are efficient to build, cohesive to experience, and scalable when needed.
