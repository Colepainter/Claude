# Thermal Suite Designer — Backyard Layout & Experience Video Prompts

**Product:** Thermal Suite Designer — an outdoor sauna + cold plunge structure.
**Style lens:** `seedance-fight-scenes` energy (camera-as-participant, 2-second hooks, tension/release, slow-mo impact frames) transposed into a premium backyard-lifestyle showcase. The dramatic engine is the **hot sauna vs. cold plunge "thermal duel"** — steam/heat vs. frost/ice.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · ~8–10s · 24fps · 1080p · synchronized audio.

---

## How to use these prompts

1. **Attach your product reference images** in Higgsfield for visual fidelity. Each prompt lists `@material[...]` placeholders — rename them to match your uploaded materials (or the in-Seedance Thermal Suite Designer product reference). Suggested set:
   - `@material[thermal_suite_exterior]` — full structure, outside
   - `@material[thermal_suite_sauna_interior]` — sauna cabin interior
   - `@material[thermal_suite_plunge]` — cold plunge tub/section
2. **Set the output to 9:16 vertical** and target 8–10 seconds.
3. **Keep the product consistent** — it's one structure containing a sauna and a cold plunge; it should not morph between shots. The reference images + product anchor handle this.
4. Swap the bracketed `[...]` notes (e.g., backyard styling) for your real footage/brand details where you want.

> Word counts are kept tight (~200–320 words) on purpose — ideal for an 8–10s generation.

## PROMPT 1 — Pool Deck

```
THERMAL SUITE DESIGNER — POOLSIDE THERMAL DUEL (9:16, 8s)
ASSETS: @material[thermal_suite_exterior], @material[thermal_suite_sauna_interior], @material[thermal_suite_plunge]
OUTPUT: 9:16 vertical, 8 seconds, 24fps, 1080p, synchronized audio

[SETTING & ATMOSPHERE] Golden-hour backyard pool deck. Still turquoise pool, wet stone coping, lounge chairs, palms throwing long shadows. Warm, luxurious, calm-before-the-rush.

[THE STRUCTURE — HERO] The Thermal Suite Designer sits at the head of the pool — a sleek modern outdoor cabin: warm cedar sauna on one side, cold plunge on the other. Hero it tall in the vertical frame.

[2-SECOND HOOK] Snap-zoom into the sauna door bursting open — a wall of steam billows straight toward camera, lit amber from within. Freeze 0.3s on the rolling steam.

[BEAT 1 — HEAT] Inside cut: glowing cedar, water hitting hot stones, steam swirling, warm 2700K light. Sweat-glow, deep exhale.

[BEAT 2 — COLD] Whip-pan to the plunge — a body breaks the icy surface, cold-water splash explodes outward in slow-motion, frost vapor curling off the water, light shifting to cool 7000K cyan.

[ESCALATION — LAYOUT REVEAL] Camera pulls back and orbits, rising slightly, to reveal the full pool deck: the structure flanked by the pool, loungers, sunset sky — showing how naturally it anchors the leisure zone.

[CLIMAX] Split energy in one frame: amber steam drifting off the cabin on the left, cool mist off the plunge on the right, pool glowing between them.

[RESOLUTION] A relaxed figure settles onto a lounger facing the structure, pool shimmering. Calm, premium, "this is the upgrade."

[CAMERA] Snap zoom, whip-pan, rising orbital pull-back, shallow depth of field.
[LIGHT & COLOR] Warm amber vs. cool cyan thermal-contrast grade; golden-hour key.
[IMPACT FX] Steam billows, cold-plunge splash, frost vapor, condensation.
[AUDIO] Steam hiss, deep plunge splash, gentle water lap, warm cinematic swell resolving soft.
```
